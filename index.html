Before You Fly

Item Details
Passport Must be valid until at least 21 December 2026 (6 months beyond entry)
Visa on Arrival Apply online before you fly at evisa.imigrasi.go.id - select the B1 30-day Tourist on Arrival visa. 500,000 IDR (~$50 AUD). Have your return flight details ready.
Bali Tourist Levy 150,000 IDR (~$15 AUD) per person. Pay at the airport kiosks on arrival (cashless - card only). Kelly will walk you through this.
Arrival Card Fill out your Indonesian Arrival Card online before you land at allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id
Travel Insurance Strongly recommended - cover medical, evacuation, and cancellation.
Spending Money We recommend bringing ~$200 AUD in cash to exchange on arrival. Use reputable money changers only - BMC in Ubud is our go-to. Most places accept card except markets.
WhatsApp WhatsApp is the main communication tool in Bali - you will need it. Make sure it's installed and working on your phone before you fly. All drivers, restaurants, and local contacts use WhatsApp. Join our group chat: Bali Crew 2026
Dietary Requirements Let Kelly know ASAP so the chef can prepare.
